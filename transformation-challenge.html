Challenge rules

Stay consistent. Stay honest. Stay in.

App tracking, weekly updates, and daily photos are compulsory for the full 90 days.

  1. 01
    Use the Fitness Gurukul App every day Workouts must be logged daily. Nutrition must be tracked on the app only.
  2. 02
    Weekly weight + body measurements Both updates are compulsory. Missing updates for more than 5 consecutive days may mean disqualification.
  3. 03
    Daily photo, Sunday set One photo every day in the same light, place, camera, pose, and distance. Every Sunday: front, side & back for comparison.
  4. 04
    No fake progress No steroids, no edited photos, no cheating. Judges’ decision is final.
  5. 05
    Social media minimums At least 4 stories every week and 1 reel every week when possible. Use #FGTransformationChallenge and #FitnessGurukul.
